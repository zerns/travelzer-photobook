About #travelzer

#travelzer is the personal travel and photography identity of Zer Cabatuan, based in Manila. He began his photography journey in 2008, exploring different genres including street, macro, and events photography. Over time, his passion for visual storytelling naturally evolved into travel, allowing him to capture moments across the Philippines and beyond.

Through his work, he adopts the name #travelzer — a personal expression of his journey as a photographer, traveler, and storyteller. Guided by curiosity and discovery, he documents places, people, and experiences through a lens shaped by authenticity and exploration. For Zer, photography remains more than a craft; it is a passion and a way to preserve the essence and stories of every destination he visits.
